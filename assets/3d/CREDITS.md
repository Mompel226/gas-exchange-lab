# The 3D chest: credits

`chest.bin` and `chest.json` are made from **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), licensed under
[CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/deed.en). BodyParts3D is one real human body, measured
by medical scanners; its parts are named from the Foundational Model of Anatomy.

**What was changed.** 75 parts were chosen (the ribs and their cartilages, the sternum, the thoracic spine, the external and
internal intercostal muscles, the diaphragm, the lungs, the trachea and bronchi) and simplified to 93,280 triangles in all. The
heart comes from the Human Body Hub's model, which is made from the same body. Movements of breathing were added: each rib
turns about its own joint with the spine, and the sternum, the cartilages, the muscles, the diaphragm and the lungs follow.

Because BodyParts3D is share-alike, **these two files stay under CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan**. They are not covered by this lab's own
licences.

The pictures of the body in the station "Physical activity and breathing" (`assets/photos/ac-*.webp`) are renders of the
Human Body Hub's model, from the same source and under the same licence.
